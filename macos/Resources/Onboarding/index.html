Plow presents

WikiSync

Your wiki, the same on every Mac you use. Edit a note here and it's there.

Private by design

Your notes can be read only on your own machines.

End-to-end encrypted
Only your machines hold the keys.
Peer to peer
Your Macs sync with each other directly. When they can't reach each other, a relay passes along encrypted data it can't read.
No server reads your files
There's no cloud copy. Plow never sees your notes or keys; the relay only passes encrypted data it can't read.

Choose your wiki

Pick the folder you keep your notes in, such as your Obsidian vault. WikiSync keeps it in sync from now on. To join a wiki another machine already syncs, use Join with a code in the menu bar.
